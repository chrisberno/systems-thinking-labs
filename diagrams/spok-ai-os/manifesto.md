# THE SPOK OS MANIFESTO

*Plain terms. How all the SPOK work connects into one shippable thing.*

---

## What we built

An AI organized like a **mind** — not a chatbot with bolt-ons, but a system with parts that each
do one job and all serve one identity:

- **SOUL** — *who it is.* Your values, taste, judgment. Owned. Permanent. Survives any model swap.
- **BRAIN** — *what it knows.* Fast recall (deepspok) + a compounding library (the vaults).
- **CAPABILITIES** — *what it can do.* Skills, routines, loops.
- **CONNECTORS** — *how it touches the world.* Senses in, acts out — through a careful membrane.

Plus an **ACTIVATION layer**: spawn it into a project, hand it a **GOAL**, and it boots knowing
*who it is, what it's for, and what it's allowed to do.*

## The realization (h/t Ben AI)

Ben AI showed that an "AI OS" doesn't have to stay an internal rig — it can be **packaged and
shipped.** Cloned. Handed to someone else. We've been building exactly that, the hard way,
internally, for months. The dots are now close enough to connect: **SPOK OS is ready to become a
thing you can hand someone.**

## The plan — connect the dots

Take everything — the four rings, the two germlines (`soul.md` + `~/SPOK/skills/`), the brain, the
disciplined membrane — and package it as a **template**:

> **clone → `cd` in → activate the harness with SPOK OS → inherit a soul, a brain, skills, and a
> careful membrane → start shipping.**

## Eat our own dog food

We prove it by **using it.** A real (dummy) **Next.js** project — `spok-os-dogfood` — built *by* a
SPOK OS agent, *from inside* the project directory, toward a clear goal. If SPOK OS makes us
dramatically faster and better at shipping it, it's real. If it doesn't, we find out before anyone
else does.

## The ladder

1. **Internal rig** — where we are.
2. **Shippable template** — this. Clone-and-go.
3. **Managed SPOKaaS** — later. The platform.
